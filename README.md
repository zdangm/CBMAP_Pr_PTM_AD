## A Human Brain Proteomic and PTM Atlas for Early Alzheimer’s Disease

This repository accompanies the study “A Human Brain Proteomic and Modification Atlas for Early Alzheimer’s Disease”, developed within the framework of the China Brain Multi-omics Atlas Project (CBMAP).

The project provides a standardized and large-scale molecular resource for investigating proteomic and post-translational modification (PTM) landscapes in human brain tissues across Alzheimer’s disease neuropathological change (ADNC).

## Project Overview

Alzheimer’s disease (AD) is characterized by progressive molecular and neuropathological alterations that precede clinical symptoms by many years. While phosphorylation of Tau has been extensively studied, the broader landscape of protein post-translational modifications in human brain tissue remains insufficiently characterized at scale.

This project was designed to:
Generate a large-scale human brain proteomic and PTM reference dataset
Systematically profile phosphorylation, ubiquitination, acetylation, and other PTMs
Anchor molecular analyses to neuropathological staging (ADNC) rather than clinical diagnosis
Enable mechanistic exploration beyond classical Tau- and amyloid-centered frameworks
